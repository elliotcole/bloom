Gestures · whole bloom, small steps

-calm: merge a note, soften, pitches inward
=intensify: split a note, louder, pitches outward
'vary: a little of everything moves
{ }merge two notes / split one in half

Rhythm only splits and merges, so the total length and your durations (halves and doubles) survive.

A bloom is five lists

pitch (a sub-list is a chord) · time to the next note, in beats · velocity (0 = rest) · channel · intervals. Each list can have its own length.

Phasing

Lists of different lengths cycle on their own: 5 pitches over 4 velocities shift the accent each time round. You hear it in the loop and pulsar; space always starts every list together. L shows a cursor per list.

Home or mode?

Home keys act on whole notes or the whole bloom; a mode applies the same verb to one list. s at home scrambles notes with their rhythm; t then s scrambles only the rhythm.

Palette

The distinct values a list already uses: your durations, dynamic levels, channels, interval steps. For pitch, the palette is the scale. “Step” keys move along your own vocabulary, so a groove of 1, ½, ¼ stays in halves and doubles.

How a freeze works

Switching one on takes a snapshot of what the list uses now. Anything outside it then snaps to the nearest allowed value: on every play, and after every change while a loop or pulsar runs.

Snapping rewrites the bloom; unfreezing doesn't undo it. Freeze again for a new snapshot.

Pins are per event, not per list: a pinned node keeps all its values. Sorts and rotations flow around it; a new bloom (n) grows around it.

Undo vs save points

cmdz steps back one change at a time. optp marks a place you like; wander as far as you want and optP jumps straight back. The notice top-left names each change for 5 s.

Breeze

# $ move only some values a step. Notes that just moved are likelier to move again, so repeated presses drift a line rather than shuffle the whole thing.

Intervals

Each step adds notes above (+) or below (−) the note it falls on, at playback; 0 adds nothing. They count semitones or scale steps (%). The list cycles at its own length, so harmony phases too. * writes them into the notes as chords.

Moving through keys

K slant jumps to a near-neighbour scale on any root and avoids the last few it visited, so it wanders outward.

[ ] travel steadily: one scale note moves a semitone per press, C → G → D… sharpward or the reverse.

Pivot and pitch classes

( ) turn the melody upside down while the highest (or loudest) note stays put.

In pitch mode h H remove or add one pitch class; the scale follows.
